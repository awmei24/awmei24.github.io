turn 01 — three directions

Redesign directions for amanda wang mei

Three distinct visual systems — all preserving your cream + sage palette and your real content (about · work · hobbies · writing · contact), each adding complementary color and a stronger point of view. Every direction shows a desktop homepage and a mobile adaptation. Reference an option by its badge (e.g. 1b) in chat.

1A

The Garden Almanac

An editorial field-journal. Big warm serif headlines, monospace chapter numbers, hairline ruled indexes like an old almanac. Quiet and literary — but confident and richly textured, not empty. Terracotta clay accent nods to your ceramics.

Newsreader · Space Mono · Plus Jakarta Sans

The Handmade Zine

Bold, characterful, unapologetically personal — the indie-web made by hand. Chunky Bricolage type, sticker cards with hard offset shadows, tilted tape-and-polaroid elements, doodle marks. This is the most playful and most distinctive of the three.
